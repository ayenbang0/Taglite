THE RUN

Win a duel, pick 1 of 3 upgrades, face tougher opponents. You also get picks during each duel. Lose once and the run is over.

Your side is randomised every duel.

THE TWO SIDES

Hunters get the legs — +5% speed, +10% sprint, -5% dash cooldown.

Prey get the toolkit — -10% ability cooldown.

MODES

Classic — 1v1, 2:00, upgrade at 1:00.

Zombie (every 3rd) — 6 fighters, 5:00, upgrades at 2:00 and 4:00. The tagged join the horde; you keep playing after you turn.

Freeze (every 5th) — 3 hunt 4, 5:00, upgrades at 2:00 and 4:00. Stand on a frozen ally to thaw them.

Every AI drafts its own build, so late runs get genuinely varied.
